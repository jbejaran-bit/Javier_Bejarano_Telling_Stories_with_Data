## About Me

Hi! I'm Javier Bejarano Jimenez, originally from Spain. I'm currently pursuing a Master's in Information Systems Management, with a focus on Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, at Carnegie Mellon University.

Before CMU, I studied Computer Science and Mathematics at Allegheny College. I'm interested in data science, machine learning, and software development. I enjoy building projects that solve real problems and make technology more useful.

Outside of school, I enjoy playing competitive tennis and working on personal projects.

## What I Hope to Learn

I want to improve how I communicate information through data. I have experience with programming and data analysis, but I know that finding interesting results is only part of the challenge. Being able to explain them clearly is just as important.

In this course, I hope to learn how to choose better visualizations, use colors more effectively, and create stories that people can understand without needing a technical background.

After graduation, I would like to work in data science, AI, or another technical role where I can apply these skills to real-world problems.
